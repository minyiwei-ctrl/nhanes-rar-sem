## Rest-activity rhythm

| Mplus | Analytic file | Created by | Definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| `IV` | `IV` | script 5 | Intradaily variability. Fragmentation of the rest-activity cycle: the ratio of the mean squared difference between successive hours to the overall variance, computed on the hourly-binned MIMS profile averaged across valid days. The von Neumann ratio, bounded [0, 4]; higher values mean a more broken-up day. Dimensionless. |
| `IS` | `IS` | script 5 | Interdaily stability. Reproducibility of the 24-hour pattern across days, as the between-hour share of total variance in the hourly profile (variance-decomposition form, which stays within [0, 1] when not every hour is observed on every day). Higher values mean a more regular rhythm. |
| `RA` | `RA` | script 5 | Relative amplitude, (M10 - L5) / (M10 + L5), computed at minute resolution. Strength of the day-night contrast, bounded [0, 1]; higher values mean a stronger rhythm. |
| `—` | `M10` | script 5 | Mean MIMS over the most active continuous 600 minutes, wrapping past midnight. Characterised but not modelled as an exposure: it correlates 0.92 with total daily volume (R2 = 0.78 on log volume), so a volume-adjusted M10 has no clean interpretation. |
| `—` | `L5` | script 5 | Mean MIMS over the least active continuous 300 minutes. Not modelled as an exposure: r = -0.88 with relative amplitude, which is derived from it. |
| `IV_Q` | `IV_Q` | script 9 | Quintiles of intradaily variability (1-5), computed within adults on observed values. A sensitivity analysis against the skew of the continuous measure. |

## Derivation rules

**Accelerometry validity.** A minute counts when the quality flag PAXQFM is zero, the wear classification PAXPREDM is wake-wear or sleep-wear, and MIMS is observed. A day counts when it has at least 600 valid minutes; a participant counts with at least 3 valid days.

**Sentinel values.** PAXMTSM = -0.01 marks "not computable" and is set to missing, not clipped to zero. Treating it as zero movement passed the quality filter for 58.9% of affected minutes and injected spurious rest.

**Minute of day.** PAXSSNMP is a session sample counter at 80 Hz, not a minute-of-day index. Clock position is (session minute - day-2 start) modulo 1440.

**Activity thresholds.** MVPA is MIMS >= 19.614; sedentary is MIMS < 1.05; the peak intensity summary averages the 30 highest minutes of a day.

**Biological age.** Klemera-Doubal, internally calibrated, from 10 markers: albumin, lymph, mcv, glucose_mmol, rdw, creat, alp, wbc, hba1c, totchol.

**Standardisation.** Every variable written to the Mplus data file is z-standardised, so the reported STDYX coefficients are per standard deviation of both exposure and outcome. Standardisation is within each model sample, so the conversion to natural units differs by analysis cell; the Scale sheet of Tables 2 and 3 gives the factors.

**Missing-value code.** Missing is written as -9999 in the Mplus file.
